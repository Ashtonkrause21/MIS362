Learning Objectives

This applied review follows Summative10 and reinforces how different AI-enabled systems and emerging technologies could support the SmartBean capacity decision.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Distinguish analytics, rules, predictive AI, generative AI, sensors, robotics, models, and agents.
  2. Compare descriptive analysis, a predefined rule, and a predictive estimate.
  3. Evaluate whether an AI response is grounded in approved information.
  4. Identify actions and decisions that require human approval.
  5. Recommend a controlled technology pilot with benefits, risks, measures, and controls.

Assignment Overview

SmartBean faces long lines, full seating, walk-in and mobile-order conflicts, and an approaching lease decision. Management wants evidence before selecting a technology or making a large commitment.

Download and open Formative10_SmartBean_AI_EmergingTech.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative10.xlsx.
