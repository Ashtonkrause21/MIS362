Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to evaluate how AI-enabled systems and emerging technologies could help SmartBean address growth, customer congestion, limited seating, and an upcoming lease decision.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Distinguish predictive AI, generative AI, rules-based systems, computer vision, robotics, and AI agents.
  2. Explain the difference between an AI model and an AI agent.
  3. Explain how retrieval-augmented generation can use approved organizational documents.
  4. Identify consequential decisions that must retain human approval.
  5. Compare cloud and network-hosted models using usefulness, speed, privacy, and resource requirements.
  6. Recommend an AI-enabled system or emerging technology for a defined SmartBean problem.
  7. Identify the data, infrastructure, evidence, expected benefit, and risk associated with the recommendation.

SmartBean Case — Growth, Capacity, and Technology Decisions

SmartBean Coffee has operated successfully for several years from a leased storefront. The location includes a service counter, a small food-preparation area, limited storage, and seating for approximately 35 customers.

Business has grown steadily because of increased walk-in traffic, repeat customers, mobile orders, local events, and customers who remain in the shop to study, work, or meet with others. During the busiest morning and afternoon periods, customers frequently wait in long lines, available seating fills quickly, and employees have difficulty preparing walk-in and mobile orders at the same time.

The current location is leased, and the lease will expire within the next eighteen months. The landlord has indicated that the rent will increase if SmartBean renews the lease. The existing building cannot be expanded easily, and the parking, storage, electrical capacity, network equipment, and food-preparation space may not support continued growth.

SmartBean management is considering several alternatives:

  • Renew the lease and redesign the existing location.
  • Lease a larger location nearby.
  • Open a second, smaller location.
  • Add a drive-through, pickup-only, or mobile-order location.
  • Use technology to improve customer flow and increase capacity at the current location.
  • Use a combination of physical expansion and new technology.

Management wants to use sales, customer, transaction, scheduling, and operational data rather than relying only on intuition. It is also interested in mobile ordering, customer-counting sensors, digital menus, self-service kiosks, scheduling systems, predictive analytics, generative AI, and AI agents.

Management is concerned about cost, customer privacy, employee acceptance, system reliability, security, accessibility, environmental impact, and whether the proposed technologies would actually solve the capacity problem.

Your role: Assist SmartBean management in evaluating how information systems, AI-enabled systems, and emerging technologies could help the organization understand and respond to its growth and capacity problem.
