SmartBean capacity case — adapted from Summative10 for a bounded classroom test
SmartBean has approximately 35 customer seats. Its lease expires within eighteen months. The landlord has indicated a rent increase. Expansion of the existing building is difficult, and parking, storage, electrical, network and preparation-space capacity may constrain growth.
Management can investigate renewing and redesigning, relocating, opening another location, or improving customer flow with technology.
This excerpt supplies no exact new rent, relocation budget, measured queue times, profit forecast, or approved investment decision. Management must gather evidence and decide. A language-model recommendation is not authorization to commit funds or sign a lease.
