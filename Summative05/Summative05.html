Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to understand how organizations convert data into reliable information. You will interpret data structures, examine relational queries, investigate a real organizational data-management problem, recommend governance controls, and distinguish authoritative information sources from AI-generated responses.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Identify entities, attributes, data types, primary keys, and relationships.
  2. Explain how relational tables and queries provide useful business information.
  3. Use Microsoft Access to examine related tables and query results.
  4. Explain why data quality, metadata, ownership, access, retention, privacy, and authoritative sources matter.
  5. Compare relational databases, big-data and analytics tools, and general-purpose AI systems.
  6. Recommend appropriate data-governance controls for an organizational problem.

Questions 1 through 10 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. Respond directly to every part of each question. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.

Exercise 1-Data Fundamentals and Modeling

 

Organizations need well-defined data to support accurate investigations, claims decisions, and reporting.

Design the basic data structure for a vehicle-theft database used by a law-enforcement agency or insurance company. The database should provide reliable information about stolen vehicles and theft incidents to investigators or claims adjusters.

Identify three useful attributes. For each attribute, state an appropriate data type and explain briefly why the attribute is needed. Then determine whether one of those attributes can serve as a reliable primary key or whether the database should use a separate IncidentID field.
